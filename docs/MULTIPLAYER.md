Online mode labels describe supported modes; this presentation review did not run a simultaneous two-client server test or verify private invitation links. Local play is explicitly labeled so players do not expect a remote server.

A race with multiple karts, computer opponents, a leaderboard, or a sports theme does not establish multiplayer. Other games remain untagged until there is evidence of human multiplayer. Unverified candidates such as Platform Party remain outside the playable collection regardless of their category.

The homepage section, Browse Games Multiplayer tab, card badges, and tag search share the same metadata. Preserve each game's other fitting categories.
